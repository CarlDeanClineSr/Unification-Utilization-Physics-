# Macro Quantum as Foam Confirmation: LUFT and the 2025 Nobel Prize

**Context:**  
The 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics (Clarke, Devoret, Martinis) recognized the experimental demonstration of macroscopic quantum tunneling and energy quantization in superconducting circuits. Billions of electrons (Cooper pairs) tunnel as a single quantum entity across a Josephson junction, creating measurable voltage and quantized energy levels.

**LUFT Perspective:**  
- **Foam/Lattice Model:** LUFT posits that all physical systems, from micro to macro, are built on a modulated foam or discrete lattice. "Foam density" controls the probability and character of transitions.
- **Quantum Tunneling:** In LUFT, a Josephson junction is a "foam bottleneck." Macroscopic tunneling is a collective patch crossing the barrier, explained by local foam modulation.
- **Energy Quantization:** Discrete energy levels in circuits are lattice states; transitions are foam "jumps."


- **Validation:** The Nobel Prize confirms LUFT: quantum foam is not just micro—macroscopic systems (grams of matter) display it.
